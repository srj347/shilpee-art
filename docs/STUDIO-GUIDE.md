# Studio guide

Studio is where you manage your website. It works best on your phone.

## Open Studio

1. Go to **shilpeemaitra.com/admin**.
2. Enter your email and tap **Email me a sign-in link**.
3. Open the email and tap **Sign in to Studio**. The link works once, for 15 minutes.
4. You stay signed in for 30 days on that phone.

Tip: in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. Studio then opens like an app.

## Add a work (about 3 minutes)

1. Tap **Add work**.
2. Tap **Add photos** and choose 3 to 6 photos. They upload while you type.
3. Tap a photo to say what it shows: **Main**, **Other view**, **Close-up**, **In a room** or **In progress**.
4. Fill in **Title**, **Height cm** and **Width cm**. The year is filled in for you.
5. Type the **Medium**, for example "Acrylic and watercolour on canvas".
   Use the words acrylic, watercolour, ink or mixed media, so visitors can filter by them.
6. Choose the **Status** and the **Price**, or choose **Price on request**.
7. Write **The story** in a few lines. You can paste your Instagram caption.
8. For prints, turn on **Sell prints of this work** and tap a preset, for example **+ Paper, A-sizes**. Change the prices if you need to.
9. Tap **Put on website**. The work shows on the website at once.

Not ready yet? Tap **Save as draft**. Only you can see a draft. Tap **Preview** to see the real page.

If a call interrupts you, nothing is lost: Studio keeps your unsaved work on the phone and offers it back.

## Photos that look like the painting

- Photograph in daylight, near a window, with no flash.
- Hold the phone straight in front of the painting. Fill the frame with the painting.
- Take one close-up with light from the side, so the texture shows.
- Take one photo of the work on a wall in a room.
- Do not use filters.
- For a watercolour on white paper, tap the photo and turn on **Show on grey mount**.
- If a room photo is made on a computer, turn on **Digital mock-up**. The website then labels it "Digital preview".

## Change a work quickly

In **Works**, tap **⋯** next to a work:

- **Mark as sold**, **Mark as reserved** or **Mark as available**
- **Take off website** or **Put on website**
- **Show first on Home** — the big painting at the top of the Home page
- **Duplicate** — start a new work with the same details (useful for a series)
- **Delete** — Studio asks first. A deleted work can be restored by your developer.

Tap **☆** to add a work to **Selected works** on the Home page.

To change the order of works on the website, tap **Reorder**, drag **☰**, then tap **Save order**.

## Upcoming works

1. Add the work with the status **Upcoming**.
2. Add the expected month and a short note from the studio.
3. People can tap **Register interest**. Studio lists them on the work's page.
4. When the work is finished, change the status to **Available** and write to those people first.

## Enquiries

- Every enquiry comes to your email, and to **Enquiries** in Studio. The red number shows new ones.
- To answer, reply to the email, or tap **Reply by email** or **WhatsApp** in Studio.
- Tap **Mark as replied** when you have answered. Use **Private note** for your own notes.
- The visitor gets a short thank-you email from you straight away, with the promise you set in Settings.

## Settings

- **About you**: your sentence for the Home page, your About text, portrait and studio photos, exhibitions.
- **Contact**: public email, WhatsApp number, Instagram link, and how soon you reply.
- **Send new enquiries to**: the emails that receive enquiries.
- **Defaults**: certificate text, shipping, packing and print times used on every work page.

Tap **Save settings**. The website shows the changes at once.
